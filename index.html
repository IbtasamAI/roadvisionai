Calibration – the most important step

Drag the four amber corners so they sit on a rectangle of flat road you can measure (e.g. lane edges and two painted marks, or cones). Corner order: bottom-left, bottom-right, top-right, top-left. Then enter the real width and length in metres. Speed accuracy depends on how exact these two numbers are.

Assisted distance & automatic zone

Experimental geometry estimate, not AI depth sensing. Enter the physical phone mounting height, downward camera tilt and approximate vertical field of view (FOV). Requires a level road. Keep manual calibration for reliable results.

Manual zone remains fully editable.

Accuracy & limits

Speed is computed from a camera-to-road calibration, not from radar or laser. Typical error is a few km/h when the zone is measured carefully, the phone is on a tripod and the vehicle is tracked for at least one second. It is not a certified or legal speed measurement. Plates are not read automatically – annotate them by hand in Records.

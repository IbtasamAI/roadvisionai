RoadVision AI - deploy on Netlify
1. Drag this whole folder (or the zip's contents) onto https://app.netlify.com/drop  - or connect it to Git (netlify.toml included, publish = ".").
2. Open the https URL on your phone (HTTPS is required for camera, GPS and motion sensors). Tap "Start camera & sensors" and allow permissions.
3. Put the phone on a tripod, calibrate the zone (drag 4 corners, enter real width/length in metres), then measure.
Detection model weights download once on first use (needs internet) and are cached on the device.
